In June 2024 my band Aimai Mirai had the opportunity to perform with any video playing on stage in the background. I took this opportunity to create a motion graphic video to accompany our performance, which would become the first time my DMA work would be made specifically to be displayed in a physical space. I aimed to create a short under 5 minutes looping video that would fit the aesthetics and mood of the performance, which proved difficult due to lack of music/audio synchronizations and arbitrary changes.
